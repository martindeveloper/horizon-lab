Scognamiglio et al. (2026), Nature Astronomy, An ultra-high-resolution map of
(dark) matter; Supplementary Data 3, JWST E-mode convergence FITS grid.

Blackbody colour
Tanner Helland (2012), temperature-to-RGB approximation, based on Mitchell
Charity's CIE 1964 10-degree blackbody colour data. Adapted to GLSL and normalized
RGB; an approximate display mapping, not a calibrated spectrum.
https://tannerhelland.com/2012/09/18/convert-temperature-rgb-algorithm-code.html
https://tannerhelland.com/code.html
The BSD-2-Clause notice is reproduced in /credits#credits-licence-texts.

CMB temperature reconstructions
Planck Collaboration: Planck 2018 PR3 SMICA R3.00 full-mission component map,
served by NASA/IPAC IRSA. Planck is an ESA science mission with instruments
and contributions directly funded by ESA Member States, NASA, and Canada.
https://irsa.ipac.caltech.edu/data/Planck/release_3/all-sky-maps/matrix_cmb.html
Atacama Cosmology Telescope Collaboration: ACT DR6.02 + Planck NILC
blackbody-temperature map with thermal SZ deprojected, and published footprint
and inpainting masks. Method: Coulton et al., Phys. Rev. D 109, 063530 (2024).
We acknowledge the use of the Legacy Archive for Microwave Background Data
Analysis (LAMBDA), part of the High Energy Astrophysics Science Archive Center

the NASA Goddard Space Flight Center.
https://lambda.gsfc.nasa.gov/product/act/act_dr6.02/act_dr6.02_nilc_info.html

colour scale and spherical projection; it does not claim native survey resolution.
