## 2026-10-23

### New labs

- **Giants**: a true-scale ladder of 25 bodies, from a city-sized neutron star past the largest stars to black-hole horizons.
  - Put any two bodies side by side, or see all of them at once as dots on a log scale.
  - Surfaces are physical: colour from temperature, limb darkening, flowing granulation, Vega and Achernar flattened by their spin, and outflows from red supergiants.
  - Every star sits on a shared HR diagram.
- **The Sun**: one star in eight views.
  - Cut it open to see the interior from the standard solar model.
  - Switch the light: white light, Hα, extreme ultraviolet, eclipse or magnetogram.
  - Fly over sunspots drawn from a real solar photograph, with the active regions observed in May 2024.
  - Watch a flux rope reconnect and erupt, and follow the solar cycle.
  - Ride along with Parker Solar Probe past the corona's edge.

### New experiences

- **Voyage**: fly a ship from the Moon to the edge of the reachable universe at true scale. It has three drives (Voyager, light sail, torch ship), a ship clock against an Earth clock, and a sky that aberrates and Doppler-shifts at speed.

### Spacecraft in 3D

- Voyager 1 and 2, Pioneer 10 and 11, New Horizons, Parker Solar Probe, Hubble, Chandra and Webb as NASA 3D models at their true size.
- Fly up to them in the Solar system, meet Voyager 1 in Deep Field's drift, and park beside a probe in Voyage.

### Solar system

- Dwarf planets: Ceres, Haumea, Makemake and Eris.
- True-size close-ups: fly up to any body, with a double tap as the shortcut.

### Deep Field

- A cosmic atlas from real surveys (Cosmicflows-4, DESI DR1, CLASSIX), shown as data, illustration or both.
- The microwave background drawn from the Planck and ACT maps.
- The dark universe: the Milky Way's dark halo, the COSMOS-Web lensing map and an expansion view.
- Portrait framing, so the whole sky fits on a phone.

### Black holes and Stellar spectra

- Black holes render faster while the camera only orbits.
- Stellar spectra places its stars on the shared HR diagram.

### Everywhere

- A guided tour of the controls in every lab, behind the `?` button.
- One view picker in every lab; on small screens the toolbar folds into "More tools".
- Figures open in a zoomable, resizable dialog.
- Body cards open the same body in the other labs.
- Two more ambient tracks ("Vast Empty Space", "The Star Rings"), plus previous-track and media-key control.
- Catalogue tiles with layered parallax artwork.
- Czech: the right plural form beside every figure.

### Science audit

- Every figure now comes from one sourced catalogue.
