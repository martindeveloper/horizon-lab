## Open flags

- Planetesimals by ~0.1 Myr and Moon-mass embryos by ~1 Myr (`orrery/parameters.ts:16`, `orrery/parameters.ts:18`): consistent with reviews; no primary text was readable.
- Moon Cassini angle 1.5424° (`toolkit/moons.ts:75`): IAU amplitudes give 1.542–1.544°; the fourth decimal (Eckhardt) was not opened.
- Halley's 2.2-day spin (`toolkit/figures.ts:110`): Belton et al. 1991 model a 3.69 d precession with a 7.1 d long-axis spin; the drawn period is a labelled teaching choice.
- Saturn B-ring albedo 0.5 used to scale ring colours (`toolkit/rings.ts:26`): no primary opened.
- Stellar photometry flags above: values kept as the combined light a naked eye sees, or where SIMBAD lacks data.
- Perseus Cluster cz 5,366 km/s (`orrery/cosmos.ts`, `WEB_SKY.perseus`): Struble & Rood 1999 seen only as quoted; NED gives NGC 1275 itself 5,297 km/s, which moves the distance by about 1 %.
- Narrative history without numbers (Kant and Laplace, aluminium-26 and a nearby massive star, Arrokoth) was not treated as a measurement.
