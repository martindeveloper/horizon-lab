- Moon Cassini angle 1.5424° (`toolkit/moons.ts:75`): IAU amplitudes give 1.542–1.544°; the fourth decimal (Eckhardt) was not opened.

- Saturn B-ring albedo 0.5 used to scale ring colours (`toolkit/rings.ts:26`): no primary opened.
- Stellar photometry flags above: values kept as the combined light a naked eye sees, or where SIMBAD lacks data.
- Narrative history without numbers (Kant and Laplace, aluminium-26 and a nearby massive star, Arrokoth) was not treated as a measurement.
