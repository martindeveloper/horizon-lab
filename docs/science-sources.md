# Science source ledger

Crossref, JPL SSD/Horizons, NAIF, PDS, NSSDC, SIMBAD, VizieR). Where a publisher blocked access,
the arXiv copy or the archived data were read instead; the note says so. The Orrery and Deep Field

| verified        | Matches the opened source at the precision shown.                                   |
| derived         | Computed from verified inputs; the note gives the arithmetic.                       |
| teaching-choice | A deliberate model or display value, labelled as such in the app or code.           |
| **flag**        | Not confirmed in an opened source; kept and listed under [Open flags](#open-flags). |

Unit constants used for derivations: GM☉ = 1.32712440041 × 10²⁰ m³ s⁻² (DE430, TDB; the IAU 2015 B3
nominal (GM)☉ = 1.3271244 × 10²⁰ m³ s⁻² is what the code uses), so
GM☉/c² = 1.4766 km and GM☉/c³ = 4.9255 μs; 1 kpc = 3,261.56 ly; 1 au = 149,597,870.7 km (IAU 2012 B2).

## Open flags

- Planetesimals by ~0.1 Myr and Moon-mass embryos by ~1 Myr (`orrery/parameters.ts`: `PLANETESIMALS_AFTER`, `EMBRYOS_AFTER`): consistent with reviews; no primary text was readable.
- Moon Cassini angle 1.5424° (`toolkit/moons.ts`): IAU amplitudes give 1.542–1.544°; the fourth decimal (Eckhardt) was not opened.
- Halley's 2.2-day spin (`toolkit/figures.ts`): Belton et al. 1991 model a 3.69 d precession with a 7.1 d long-axis spin; the drawn period is a labelled teaching choice.
- Saturn B-ring albedo 0.5 used to scale ring colours (`toolkit/rings.ts`): no primary opened.
- Stellar photometry flags above: values kept as the combined light a naked eye sees, or where SIMBAD lacks data.
- Perseus Cluster cz 5,366 km/s (`cosmos/cosmos.ts`, `WEB_SKY.perseus`): Struble & Rood 1999 seen only as quoted; NED gives NGC 1275 itself 5,297 km/s, which moves the distance by about 1 %.
- Deep Field's "On Earth" boundaries (`deep-field/depth.ts`: `EARTH_THEN`): standard figures, sources not reopened.
- The probes' launch and boundary-crossing dates (`solar/probes.ts`: `PROBES`): standard mission dates, sources not reopened; the crossing distances read from the Horizons tracks on those dates match the published 94, 84, 121.6 and 119 au.
- Narrative history without numbers (Kant and Laplace, aluminium-26 and a nearby massive star, Arrokoth) was not treated as a measurement.
